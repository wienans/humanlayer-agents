# Product Design synthesizes resolved decisions on the tracker

Source: [Define the problem and behavior design contract](https://github.com/wienans/humanlayer-agents/issues/5)

Product Design is a Design Artifact that owns the approved problem-and-behavior contract for an Effort, synthesized from resolved Wayfinder decisions, Evidence, and Domain Documentation rather than produced by a fresh PRD interview. It materializes as a separate `design:product` tracker record for Large work and nontrivial Medium work, keeps concise Product intent on the Effort root otherwise, links winning prototype visuals from their throwaway branch rather than committing HTML to the main branch, verifies implicitly when a human starts the next workflow step, and returns only consequential gaps to the Decision Map as new Decision Tickets. This replaces the HumanLayer PRD interview with a no-repeat synthesis model so resolved decisions are never re-asked while the contract stays language-neutral and tracker-neutral.

The size-named workflow and materialization guidance in this decision is superseded by [ADR-0008](0008-use-rpi-and-wayfinder-workflows.md); its Product Design authority and synthesis decisions remain accepted.

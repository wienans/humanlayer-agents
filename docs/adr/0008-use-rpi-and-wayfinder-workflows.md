# Use RPI and Wayfinder workflows

Source: [Define skill topology, invocation, and adapters](https://github.com/wienans/humanlayer-agents/issues/12)

The suite supports two explicit-human-invocation paths: the RPI (Research, Plan, Implement) Workflow begins with Research Questions and Research, while the Wayfinder Workflow dynamically charts a Decision Map for work of any size and delegates Research Questions to subagents as needed. Both converge on Product Design, Technical Design, Delivery Tickets, implementation, PR description, and review; each phase stops for the human to invoke the next, and Research always requires explicit human invocation. A standalone Reconnaissance phase and Small workflow are deferred so the first suite can merge the proven Matt and HumanLayer workflows without prematurely designing a reduced path.

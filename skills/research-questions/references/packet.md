# Research Packet

Use this body for an RPI Research record or return the same packet to Wayfinder.

```markdown
Role: Research

# Research: {neutral current-state subject}

## Research Questions

1. {Question about existing behavior, ownership, interaction, or constraints.}
2. {Question about another current-state concern.}

## Named Sources

- `{exact/path/or/directory/}` - {what may be inspected; directories include descendants}
- `{dependency or repository}` - {named version or relevant source scope}
- <https://exact.example/source> - {official material in scope}

## Source Boundaries

- Inspect only the named sources and their stated descendant scope.
- Retain unavailable or insufficiently evidenced answers as unanswered.
```

Do not include the originating request, parent Effort destination, Decision Map destination, desired outcome, proposed design, implementation plan, alternatives under consideration, or evaluation criteria that imply a preferred answer.

Questions remain neutral when any answer could be useful. Prefer "How does the current queue establish delivery order?" over "Why does the queue not preserve priority?" Prefer exact pointers over instructions to search the whole repository.
